{{APP_NAME}} Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

{{APP_NAME}} lets couples create a wedding "Dream Wishlist" and lets their guests contribute towards it. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • From couples: your name, your partner's name, your UPI ID, and the dreams (titles and target amounts) you create.
  • From guests: the name you type, the gift amount, the dream you chose, and the optional message you write. These are shown on the couple's wishlist page.
  • Payment details: online payments are handled by our payment partner, Razorpay. Card, bank and UPI credentials are entered with Razorpay and are never seen or stored by us. We receive only the payment status, amount and payment ID.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly for abuse prevention (rate limiting). We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

To show the wishlist and its progress, to process and record gifts, to let couples confirm direct UPI gifts, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your data.

Who can see what

Anyone with a wishlist link can see the couple's names, UPI ID, dreams, progress, and the names, amounts and messages of confirmed gifts. Share your link only with people you want to see this.

Sharing

We share data only with Razorpay (to process payments) and our hosting provider (to run the service), or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Couples can delete their wishlist and all associated data at any time from the app (Menu → Delete wishlist), or by following the steps on our data deletion page. Payment records may be retained by Razorpay and by us where required for accounting or legal reasons.

Children

{{APP_NAME}} is not directed at children under 18.
